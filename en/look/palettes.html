Palettes

A palette holds the colors of a deck, each with a role: titles, text, backgrounds, the bottom bar.

This page covers the color roles of a palette, the five built-in palettes, how to make your own, and which palette wins when several are set.

Colors by role

A Palette does not list “the colors of the brand”; it says what each color is for. The template paints each part of a slide with the color of its role:

Role Default Paints
primary ■ #e4572e Titles and the main segment of the bottom bar.
secondary ■ #2a6fdb The end segment of the bottom bar.
dark ■ #404040 Background of the cover and of the logo-only closing slide.
closing_bg ■ #363e45 Background of the closing slide.
light ■ #f5f5f5 Background of dividers and content slides.
text ■ #343e46 Body text on light backgrounds.
muted ■ #6b7280 Subtitles, labels and captions on light backgrounds.
on_dark ■ #f5f5f5 Text on dark backgrounds.
panel #ffffff Background of the box that holds a chart or an animation.

Three optional roles add variety:

  • section_colors: colors that Section slides take in turn by their number (section 01 the first, 02 the second, and so on).
  • bar_colors: colors of the bottom bar in equal segments, instead of primary and secondary.
  • icon_color: color of named icons; by default they take the title color.

Plots and animations on a slide also follow the palette: their series use section_colors (or primary and secondary), their labels use text and muted, and their background is panel. See Plot and Manim.

The built-in palettes

banners ships five palettes. ORANGE is the default one.

Your own palette

Create a Palette with the roles you want to change; the others keep their default value:

from banners import Cover, Palette

brand = Palette(primary="#a78bfa", secondary="#f472b6", dark="#1a0533")
Cover(title="Custom palette", subtitle="Three colors change the whole slide.", palette=brand)

section_colors gives each section its own color:

from banners import Palette, Section

rainbow = Palette(section_colors=("#e4572e", "#2a6fdb", "#2e9e5b"))
Section(title="Each section takes the next color", number="02", palette=rainbow)

Where a palette is set, and which one wins

A palette can be set in three places. When more than one is set, the most specific one wins:

  1. On a slide, with palette=: only that slide.
  2. For the deck, with configure(palette=...): every slide after the call.
  3. In the template, with Template(palette=...): the default.
from banners import BLUE, GREEN, Section, configure

configure(palette=BLUE)                        # every slide is blue...
Section(title="Highlights", palette=GREEN)     # ...except this one

A palette always replaces the one below it as a whole: the roles are not mixed. Palette(primary="#0f766e") keeps the default values for every other role, not the values of the template’s palette.

TipReadable titles on any background

When a title color would be hard to read on its background (a contrast ratio under 3:1), the template uses a readable color instead. To keep exact brand colors, create the template with auto_contrast=False.
